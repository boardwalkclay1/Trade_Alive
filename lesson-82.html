Stage 82 – Pattern Challenges

Challenges Build Real Skill

These challenges test your speed, accuracy, and discipline. You will identify patterns, confirm them, and classify them under time pressure.

This is where you learn to trust your training.

Challenge Types

  • Timed Identification: Identify 10 patterns in 60 seconds
  • Blind Trend Challenge: Identify trend before pattern
  • Location Challenge: Label support/resistance first
  • Confirmation Challenge: Choose the correct confirmation candle
  • Failure Challenge: Spot traps and failed breakouts

These drills sharpen your instincts.
