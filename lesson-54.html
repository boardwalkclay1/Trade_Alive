Stage 54 – Triangles

What a Triangle Is

A triangle is a compression pattern where price forms lower highs and higher lows — or one side stays flat while the other compresses.

Triangles show energy building before a breakout.

Types of Triangles

  • Symmetrical Triangle – neutral compression
  • Ascending Triangle – bullish bias
  • Descending Triangle – bearish bias

The breakout direction matters more than the shape.
