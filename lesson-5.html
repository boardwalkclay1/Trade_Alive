Stage 5 – Entering & Exiting Simulated Trades

From Chain to Action

Up to now, you’ve focused on direction, calls vs puts, and reading the options chain. Now you connect those ideas to actual order entry and exit—still in a simulated, educational environment.

A disciplined trader does not just click “buy” and “sell.” They know which contract they are choosing, how many contracts, at what price, and how they plan to exit.

This stage walks you through that process step by step so that later, when you see a real order ticket, it feels familiar—not intimidating.

Basic Order Ticket Elements

A simple options order ticket will usually ask you for:

  • Action: Buy to open, sell to close, etc.
  • Quantity: How many contracts you want.
  • Limit or market: Whether you set a specific price or accept the current ask.
  • Price: Your limit price if you choose a limit order.
  • Time in force: How long the order stays active (e.g., day only).

For this academy, we will focus on buy to open and sell to close, and on the difference between blindly hitting the market and using a thoughtful limit price.
