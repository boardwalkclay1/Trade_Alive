Stage 47 – News Event Candles

News Creates Chaos

News events create extreme volatility. Candles become huge, wicks explode, and price moves faster than normal. This is where emotional traders lose everything.

Disciplined traders stay out or wait for the chaos to settle.

Candle Behavior During News

  • Massive candles
  • Long wicks
  • Instant reversals
  • Fakeouts
  • Stop hunts

These candles are not signals — they are noise.
