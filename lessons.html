Your Training Path

Each stage must be completed in order. New stages unlock only after you finish the ones before them. Each lesson includes a detailed explanation, an interactive quiz, and a mock simulation that adds one more piece to your full practice trade.

Stage 1 — Discipline & Simple Direction

Unlocked

Learn why discipline and timing matter more than anything, and build your first mental model: the market can move up or down, you put money in, you want it to move your way, and then you take it out.

Stage 2 — What the Market Really Is

Locked

Understand that the market is made of real people and institutions on the other side of every contract, moving in real time. Learn what a ticker is, what a chart is, and what you are actually trading when you trade options on a ticker.

Stage 3 — Calls, Puts, and Direction

Locked

Connect your simple up-or-down model to options language: calls generally benefit when price goes up, puts when price goes down. Begin mapping the dumbed-down model to the real-world terms.

Stage 4 — Options Page Breakdown

Locked

Walk through an options page step by step: strike prices, expiration, quantity, bid, ask, and spreads—and why each one matters for discipline and execution.

Stage 5 — Entering & Exiting Simulated Trades

Locked

Practice entering and exiting calls and puts in a simulated environment. Each step maps back to the simple “money in, move, money out” model.

Stage 6 — Full Mock Trade & Emotional Control

Locked

Combine everything: discipline, direction, calls, puts, options pages, and order execution into one final simulated flow, plus questions about emotional control and preparedness. Completing this stage unlocks Chapter 2.

Stage 1 — Discipline Training

Unlocked

Establish your daily 9:00 AM ritual, maintain check-in streaks, and prove you can show up on time before learning anything else.

Stage 2 — Up vs Down (Simple Direction)

Locked

Learn the most basic model: the market can move up or down. You put money in, you want it to move your way, and you take money out. No jargon—just direction and timing.

Stage 3 — Calls = Up, Puts = Down

Locked

Connect your understanding of “up or down” to the language of options: calls for up, puts for down. Still educational and direction-focused.

Stage 4 — Options Page Breakdown

Locked

Step-by-step walkthrough of an options page: quantity, bid price, ask price, spreads, and how these elements can help or hurt you based on your discipline.

Stage 5 — Entering & Exiting Simulated Trades

Locked

Practice entering and exiting trades in a purely simulated environment. No real money, just execution practice and feedback.

Stage 6 — Emotional Control & Risk Awareness

Locked

Learn how impatience, fear, and greed sabotage traders—and how disciplined routines protect you from yourself.
