Your Training Path

Each stage must be completed in order. New stages unlock only after you finish the ones before them. This keeps your learning structured and discipline-driven.

Stage 1 — Discipline Training

Unlocked

Establish your daily 9:00 AM ritual, maintain check-in streaks, and prove you can show up on time before learning anything else.

Stage 2 — Up vs Down (Simple Direction)

Locked

Learn the most basic model: the market can move up or down. You put money in, you want it to move your way, and you take money out. No jargon—just direction and timing.

Stage 3 — Calls = Up, Puts = Down

Locked

Connect your understanding of “up or down” to the language of options: calls for up, puts for down. Still educational and direction-focused.

Stage 4 — Options Page Breakdown

Locked

Step-by-step walkthrough of an options page: quantity, bid price, ask price, spreads, and how these elements can help or hurt you based on your discipline.

Stage 5 — Entering & Exiting Simulated Trades

Locked

Practice entering and exiting trades in a purely simulated environment. No real money, just execution practice and feedback.

Stage 6 — Emotional Control & Risk Awareness

Locked

Learn how impatience, fear, and greed sabotage traders—and how disciplined routines protect you from yourself.
