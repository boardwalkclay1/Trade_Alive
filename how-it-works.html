How Discipline Academy Works

The academy is built to be simple to access and powerful to use. No complicated portals, no endless menus. Just a clear path from where you are now to where you want to be.

Step-by-Step Flow

  1. Go to the access page (paywall).
  2. Enter the email that unlocks your academy or complete payment.
  3. Once access is granted, the entire academy unlocks on this device.

Progress and access are stored locally on this device. If you switch devices, you’ll need to unlock again.

Move at Your Own Pace

You’re not rushed. You can replay lessons, rewatch breakdowns, and revisit key concepts as many times as you need. The goal is not speed—it’s mastery.

Real-Time Application

As you move through the academy, you’ll see how the concepts show up in real-time trades. This is where everything clicks: patterns, levels, options, and discipline all working together.
