Stage 43 – Trend Acceleration Candles

What Trend Acceleration Looks Like

Trend acceleration happens when a trend gains speed. Candles grow larger, wicks shrink, and price moves quickly in one direction. This is the market showing urgency — buyers or sellers pressing harder.

These candles often appear after a breakout, after compression, or after a strong pullback.

Acceleration + Moving Averages

When acceleration happens near rising moving averages in an uptrend, it’s healthy. When acceleration happens far from moving averages, it can be dangerous — overextension.

This is why discipline matters. You don’t chase acceleration — you read it.
