Stage 28 – Momentum Reversals

When Momentum Flips

Momentum reversals happen when strong buying suddenly weakens, or strong selling suddenly slows. You’ve already learned how to read candle strength — now you learn how to spot when that strength is fading.

A sequence of strong green candles followed by a small green candle with a long upper wick? Buyers losing steam. A sequence of strong red candles followed by a small red candle with a long lower wick? Sellers losing steam.

This is how you avoid chasing and stay disciplined.

Momentum Reversals + Trend

A momentum reversal against the trend is often noise. A momentum reversal with the trend can be powerful.

This is why you always start with trend and moving averages. Candles confirm — they don’t lead.
