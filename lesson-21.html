Stage 21 – Candle Patterns

Basic Candle Patterns

Candle patterns are combinations of bars that show shifts in pressure. Examples include: engulfing candles, inside bars, and rejection wicks.

These patterns help you confirm or reject trade ideas. They are not magic — they are visual evidence of buyers or sellers stepping in or backing off.

Confirmation with Discipline

A candle pattern alone is not a trade. You still need trend, moving averages, structure, and your risk plan. Candle patterns confirm your idea — they don’t create it.

This is where your mock enter/exit simulator becomes powerful. You will use candle patterns to confirm entries and manage exits with discipline.
