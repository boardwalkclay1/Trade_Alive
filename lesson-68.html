Stage 68 – Pattern + Levels Integration

Levels Define the Pattern’s Purpose

A pattern at the wrong level is meaningless. A bull flag at resistance is weak. A bull flag at support is strong. Levels give patterns their context and their power.

Levels turn drawings into setups.

Level Rules

  • Support strengthens bullish patterns
  • Resistance strengthens bearish patterns
  • Breakout levels define expansion zones
  • Necklines confirm reversals
  • Trendlines validate structure

Patterns without levels are just shapes.
