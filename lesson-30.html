Stage 30 – Exhaustion Signals

What Is Exhaustion?

Exhaustion happens when a trend runs out of energy. Buyers stop pushing. Sellers stop pressing. Candles shrink. Wicks grow. Momentum fades.

Exhaustion is not a reversal signal by itself — it is a warning. It tells you to tighten up, protect profits, and avoid emotional entries.

Exhaustion + Moving Averages

Exhaustion near a moving average can be healthy — a pause before continuation. Exhaustion far from a moving average can be dangerous — a sign of overextension.

This is why you always combine candles with trend and structure.
