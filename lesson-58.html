Stage 58 – Inverse H&S

What Inverse H&S Is

Inverse Head & Shoulders is the bullish version of the classic H&S pattern. It forms when sellers push down, fail, push lower, fail again, then push higher than before.

It shows seller exhaustion and buyer takeover.

Pattern Structure

  • Left shoulder (higher low)
  • Head (lower low)
  • Right shoulder (higher low)
  • Neckline
  • Breakout (confirmation)

The neckline break is the real signal — not the shoulders.
