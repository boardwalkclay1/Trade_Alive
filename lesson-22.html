Stage 22 – Rejection Wicks

What Rejection Wicks Tell You

A rejection wick is one of the most powerful candle signals you can learn. It shows where price tried to go — and failed. A long upper wick means buyers pushed up, but sellers slammed it back down. A long lower wick means sellers pushed down, but buyers stepped in hard.

These wicks are the fingerprints of the auction. They show where pressure shifted. They show where someone lost control. They show where someone stepped in with force.

When I was growing accounts — including the stretch where I turned $400 into $40,000 in five days — rejection wicks were one of the clearest signals I used to understand who was winning the fight.

Rejection Wicks + Trend + Discipline

A rejection wick alone is not a trade. You still need trend, structure, moving averages, and your risk plan. But a rejection wick can confirm or deny your idea.

In an uptrend, a long lower wick near a moving average can show buyers defending. In a downtrend, a long upper wick near resistance can show sellers attacking.

This is how you avoid emotional entries and stay aligned with your plan.
