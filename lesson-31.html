Stage 31 – Candle Speed & Flow

What Candle Speed Tells You

Candle speed is how fast a candle forms. A candle that fills quickly shows aggression. A candle that crawls shows hesitation. Speed is one of the most underrated signals in trading.

Fast green candles = buyers attacking. Fast red candles = sellers attacking. Slow candles = indecision or balance.

This is the heartbeat of the auction — the rhythm behind the bars.

Speed + Trend + MAs

Speed matters most when it aligns with trend and moving averages. Fast candles in the direction of trend confirm strength. Fast candles against trend often fade.

This is how you avoid emotional trades and stay disciplined.
