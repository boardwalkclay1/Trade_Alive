Stage 20 – Candle Strength & Momentum

Understanding Candle Strength

Candle strength is about how aggressively buyers or sellers moved price. A strong green candle with a full body and small wicks shows confident buying. A weak green candle with long upper wick shows hesitation.

These details matter. They help you understand whether momentum is building or fading. They help you avoid emotional entries and stay aligned with your moving averages and patterns.

Momentum & Pressure

Momentum is the speed and force behind price movement. When candles grow in size and close near their highs, buyers are applying pressure. When candles shrink or show long wicks, pressure is fading.

This connects directly to your mock enter/exit simulator. You will use candle strength to confirm entries and manage exits with discipline.
