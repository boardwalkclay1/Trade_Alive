Stage 53 – Channels

What a Channel Is

A channel is a clean, structured trend. Price moves between two parallel trendlines — either rising, falling, or sideways.

Channels show organized auction flow.

Types of Channels

  • Ascending Channel – bullish trend
  • Descending Channel – bearish trend
  • Horizontal Channel – consolidation

Channels are the cleanest way to see trend structure.
