Stage 23 – Inside Bars & Compression

What an Inside Bar Means

An inside bar forms when a candle stays completely inside the range of the previous candle. This shows compression — the market is tightening, waiting, coiling.

Inside bars are not signals by themselves. They are pauses. They show indecision. They show that buyers and sellers are temporarily balanced.

But compression often leads to expansion. When the market coils, it eventually releases.

Inside Bars + Trend = Clarity

In an uptrend, an inside bar near a moving average can be a healthy pause before continuation. In a downtrend, an inside bar near resistance can be a pause before sellers push again.

This is where your discipline matters. You don’t guess. You wait for the break. You wait for confirmation. You stay aligned with your plan.
