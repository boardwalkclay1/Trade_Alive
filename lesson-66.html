Stage 66 – Pattern + Trend Integration

Patterns Must Align With Trend

A pattern that goes against the trend is weak. A pattern that aligns with the trend is strong. Trend is the foundation — patterns are the structure built on top of it.

Trend + pattern = disciplined setup. Pattern alone = emotional guess.

Integration Rules

  • Continuation patterns must align with trend
  • Reversal patterns must form at exhaustion
  • Patterns near MAs are stronger
  • Patterns far from MAs are weaker
  • Candles confirm the idea

This is the real system — not drawings, not guesses, not emotion.
