Stage 33 – Candle Timing

Timing Is Everything

A candle at the right time means everything. A candle at the wrong time means nothing. Timing is one of the most advanced candle skills — and one of the most important.

A strong candle at market open? Normal. A strong candle at lunch hour? Weak. A strong candle near close? Powerful.

Timing reveals the rhythm of the auction.

Timing + Trend + MAs

Timing matters most when combined with trend and moving averages. A strong candle at the right time in the right place is confirmation. A strong candle at the wrong time in the wrong place is a trap.

This is how you stay disciplined and avoid emotional trades.
