Stage 41 – Volatility Spikes

What Volatility Spikes Look Like

Volatility spikes create large candles, long wicks, and fast movement. These spikes often happen during news, open, close, or major level tests.

Emotional traders get destroyed here. Disciplined traders stay patient.

Volatility + Trend + MAs

A volatility spike in trend direction can be continuation. A volatility spike against trend is often a trap. A volatility spike at a key level can be a reversal or breakout — but only with confirmation.

This is where discipline matters most.
