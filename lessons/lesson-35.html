Stage 35 – Expansion & Contraction

The Market Breathes

Candles expand and contract in cycles. Expansion is when candles grow in size — volatility increases. Contraction is when candles shrink — volatility decreases.

Expansion often follows contraction. Contraction often follows expansion. This is the rhythm of the auction.

Expansion + Trend

Expansion in the direction of trend is healthy. Expansion against trend is often a trap. Contraction near moving averages is often a pause before continuation.

This is how you stay disciplined and avoid emotional trades.
