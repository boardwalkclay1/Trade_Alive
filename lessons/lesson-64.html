Stage 64 – Pattern Timing

Timing Makes or Breaks a Pattern

A pattern is only valid when it forms at the right time in the trend. A bull flag at the top of a massive move is not the same as a bull flag early in a trend.

Timing determines whether a pattern is:

  • Continuation
  • Exhaustion
  • Trap
  • Reversal

Emotional traders ignore timing. Disciplined traders wait for it.

Good Timing vs Bad Timing

  • Good Timing: Early or mid‑trend continuation patterns
  • Bad Timing: Late‑trend patterns far from moving averages
  • Good Timing: Reversal patterns after exhaustion
  • Bad Timing: Reversal patterns in the middle of a strong trend

Timing is context — and context is discipline.
