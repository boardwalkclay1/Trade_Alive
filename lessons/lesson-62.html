Stage 62 – Pattern Failure Logic

What Pattern Failure Really Means

Pattern failure happens when price breaks a pattern in the expected direction, then instantly reverses and breaks the opposite way. This exposes emotional traders and rewards disciplined ones.

Failure is not random — it’s a shift in pressure.

Failure Requirements

  • Breakout wick
  • Snap‑back inside pattern
  • Breakout opposite direction
  • Candle confirmation

Failure is one of the strongest reversal signals in the market.
