Stage 59 – Rounded Tops & Bottoms

What Rounded Patterns Are

Rounded tops and bottoms are slow, controlled reversals. Instead of sharp rejection, price gradually shifts direction — showing a smooth transition of power.

These patterns reflect gradual exhaustion and gradual takeover.

Types

  • Rounded Top – bearish reversal
  • Rounded Bottom – bullish reversal

These patterns often form on higher timeframes and align beautifully with moving averages.
