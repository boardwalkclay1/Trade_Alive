Stage 37 – Trend Shift Candles

Candles Reveal Trend Shifts Early

Trend shifts rarely happen instantly. They build. Candles begin to weaken before the trend officially breaks. You’ll see smaller bodies, longer wicks, hesitation, and failed pushes.

A strong trend doesn’t suddenly reverse — it slows first. Candles tell you this before moving averages catch up.

Early Warning Signs

  • Strong candles become weak candles
  • Wicks appear where they didn’t before
  • Inside bars form more frequently
  • Momentum candles fail to follow through

These are the first clues that buyers or sellers are losing control.
