This Is Not a Game

Discipline Academy is not for gambling, not for chasing, not for “I feel like it” trades.

You are here to:

  • Respect risk like it’s your business.
  • Follow structure over emotion.
  • Trade patterns inside a system, not in isolation.
  • Build habits that don’t disappear when the market moves against you.

If you’re not ready to treat this seriously, stop. If you are, commit – fully.
