Why Learn to Trade

Trading is one of the few skills that can completely change your life. It teaches you how to think clearly under pressure, how to manage risk, and how to build something that no one can take from you.

Most people fail not because trading is too hard, but because they quit too early. They let emotions take over. They chase hype. They panic. They blow up. But if you stay disciplined, follow structure, and refuse to give up, trading becomes a tool that can open doors you never thought possible.

How to Enable Options Trading

Before you can trade options, you must enable options trading inside your brokerage account. Every platform asks similar questions: your experience, your goals, your risk tolerance, and your financial background.

Funding Your Account

Deposit times vary by broker:

  • Webull: Instant buying power, full funds in 3–5 days.
  • Robinhood: Instant buying power, full funds in 3–5 days.
  • Fidelity: 1–3 business days.
  • TD Ameritrade: 2–4 business days.
