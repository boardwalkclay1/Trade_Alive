Stage 13 – Support, Resistance & Liquidity

Support & Resistance: The Market’s “Memory”

Support and resistance are areas where buyers or sellers have repeatedly shown strength. These levels form because the auction has a “memory” — traders remember where price previously reacted. 

SUPPORT & RESISTANCE MAP
──────────────────────────────────────────────

Resistance (Sellers)
───────────────▼───────────────
                Price rejects here

   Consolidation / Balance
   ████████████████████████

Support (Buyers)
───────────────▲───────────────
                Price bounces here

These levels don’t guarantee reversals — they simply show where the auction has reacted before.

Trendlines: Visualizing Pressure

Trendlines help you see the slope of buying or selling pressure. They connect higher lows in an uptrend or lower highs in a downtrend.

Breakouts vs Fakeouts

A breakout happens when price moves through support or resistance with strength. A fakeout happens when price pokes through a level but quickly reverses. 

BREAKOUT
──────────────────────────────────────────────

Resistance
───────────────▼───────────────
                ▲
                │ Strong breakout
                │
                ▲

FAKEOUT
──────────────────────────────────────────────

Resistance
───────────────▼───────────────
                ▲
                │ Weak push
                ▼ Rejection back inside

Fakeouts trap emotional traders. Breakouts reward disciplined ones.

Liquidity Zones: Where Stops Live

Liquidity zones are areas where many traders place stop losses. The market often “wicks” into these zones to fill orders before moving. 

LIQUIDITY SWEEP
──────────────────────────────────────────────

Support
───────────────▲───────────────

Stops Below Support
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

▼ Liquidity Sweep
│
▲ Recovery

Interpretation:
• Price dips below support  
• Stops get triggered  
• Market snaps back up

Understanding liquidity helps you avoid emotional reactions to normal market behavior.
