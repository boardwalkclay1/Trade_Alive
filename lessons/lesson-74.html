Stage 74 – Pattern-Based Stop Placement

Stops Must Be Logical

A stop is not a guess — it is placed at the point where your trade idea is invalidated.

Stops protect discipline. No stop = emotional trading.

Stop Placement Rules

  • Continuation patterns: Stop goes below/above pattern structure
  • Reversal patterns: Stop goes beyond the extreme (head, double bottom, etc.)
  • Breakout entries: Stop goes below/above breakout level
  • Retest entries: Stop goes below/above retest candle

Stops must be placed where the pattern fails — not where you feel scared.
