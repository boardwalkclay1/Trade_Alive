Stage 52 – Wedges

What a Wedge Pattern Is

A wedge is a pattern where price compresses into a narrowing structure. Both trendlines point in the same direction — either both rising or both falling.

Wedges show pressure imbalance. One side is losing strength.

Types of Wedges

  • Rising Wedge – bearish reversal or continuation
  • Falling Wedge – bullish reversal or continuation

Rising wedge = buyers weakening. Falling wedge = sellers weakening.
