Stage 27 – Candle Confirmation for Entries & Exits

Candles Confirm Entries

You never enter a trade because of one candle. You enter because the trend, structure, and moving averages align — and then candles confirm your idea. A bullish engulfing candle at a moving average in an uptrend is confirmation. A rejection wick at resistance in a downtrend is confirmation.

Candles Confirm Exits

Candles also help you exit with discipline. A strong red candle breaking below a respected moving average can be a warning. A series of weak candles after momentum can be a sign to scale out.

This is how you use candles inside your mock enter/exit simulator — not to predict, but to confirm.
