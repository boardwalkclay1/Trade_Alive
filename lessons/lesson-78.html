Stage 78 – Trade Journaling

Journaling Creates Mastery

Journaling is where traders grow. You cannot improve what you do not measure.

Pattern‑based journaling tracks:

  • Pattern type
  • Trend alignment
  • Location
  • Entry type
  • Stop placement
  • Target logic
  • Management decisions
  • Outcome

Journaling turns experience into skill.

The Journaling Formula

  • What was the pattern?
  • Was it aligned with trend?
  • Was the location correct?
  • Did volume confirm?
  • Did candles confirm?
  • Did you follow your plan?
  • What would you improve?

Journaling is not punishment — it is refinement.
