Stage 79 – Trade Examples

Real Trades, Real Logic

Seeing patterns in theory is one thing. Seeing them in real market conditions is where mastery begins. These examples show how disciplined traders execute pattern‑based setups.

Example 1: Bull Flag Continuation

  • Trend: Uptrend
  • Location: Near rising moving averages
  • Pattern: Tight bull flag
  • Volume: Rising on breakout
  • Entry: Retest entry
  • Stop: Below flag structure
  • Target: Pole projection

Example 2: Double Bottom Reversal

  • Trend: Downtrend into exhaustion
  • Location: Major support
  • Pattern: Double bottom
  • Volume: Spike on neckline break
  • Entry: Breakout entry
  • Stop: Below second bottom
  • Target: Neckline projection
