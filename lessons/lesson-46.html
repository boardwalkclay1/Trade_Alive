Stage 46 – Reversal Zone Candles

Reversal Zones Explained

Reversal zones are areas where price has turned before — major support, major resistance, supply, demand, or psychological levels. Candles behave differently here because the auction becomes emotional.

This is where discipline matters most. Emotional traders get trapped. Disciplined traders wait for confirmation.

Candle Behavior in Reversal Zones

  • Long wicks
  • Engulfing candles
  • Inside bars
  • Failed pushes
  • Momentum shifts

These candles show pressure changing hands — buyers losing control or sellers losing control.
