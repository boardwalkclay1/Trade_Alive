Stage 75 – Pattern-Based Target Placement

Why Targets Matter

Targets define the reward side of your trade. Without a target, you’re not trading — you’re hoping. Pattern‑based targets give you:

  • Clear reward expectations
  • Consistent R:R (risk-to-reward)
  • Objective exits instead of emotional exits
  • A structured way to measure pattern performance

Target Types

Every pattern has a logical target based on its structure. These are the most reliable:

  • Measured Move Target: Use the height of the pattern projected from the breakout.
  • Structure Target: Use the next major support/resistance level.
  • Trend Target: Use the next trendline or channel boundary.
  • Volume Target: Use the point where volume historically fades.

Targets must be logical, repeatable, and tied to the pattern — not random.

Examples

1. Bull Flag

  • Target = length of the pole projected upward
  • Secondary target = next resistance

2. Double Bottom

  • Target = neckline distance projected upward
  • Secondary target = previous swing high

3. Head & Shoulders

  • Target = head-to-neckline distance projected downward
  • Secondary target = next major support
