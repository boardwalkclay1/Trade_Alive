Stage 38 – Breakouts & Fakeouts

Real Breakouts vs Fakeouts

A real breakout has strong candles, strong volume, and follow‑through. A fakeout has weak candles, hesitation, and immediate rejection.

Candles tell you the truth faster than anything else.

Breakout Candle Behavior

  • Strong body
  • Small wicks
  • Closes above the level
  • Next candle continues the move

Fakeouts often show the opposite — long wicks, hesitation, and no follow‑through.

Mini Simulation: Breakout Read
