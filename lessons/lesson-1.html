Stage 1 – Discipline & Direction

Why Discipline Comes Before Everything

Before you learn any terms, strategies, or chart patterns, you must learn how to show up. Markets reward people who are prepared, calm, and on time. They punish people who are late, impulsive, or emotional.

Discipline is not optional. It is the foundation that supports every decision you make. If you cannot commit to being prepared and on time, no amount of technical knowledge will protect you.

In this academy, your discipline is measured through routines like the 9:00 AM check-in and your willingness to complete each stage fully before moving on.

The Simplest Possible Market Model

For now, forget all the noise. Imagine the market as a simple line that can move in one of two directions from where you enter:

  • Up from your entry
  • Down from your entry

You put money in, you choose a direction, you wait, and then you take your money out. That is the core idea. Everything else is detail and language layered on top of this.

Later, when we talk about calls and puts, order types, and charts, we will always come back to this simple model: money in, movement, money out.
