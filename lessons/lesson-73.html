Stage 73 – Pattern-Based Entry Types

Entries Must Be Systematic

A pattern does not give you an entry — your system does. Entries must align with:

  • Trend
  • Location
  • Structure
  • Volume
  • Candle confirmation

Emotional entries destroy accounts. Systematic entries build consistency.

Entry Types

  • Breakout Entry: Enter on the breakout candle
  • Retest Entry: Enter on the retest of the breakout level
  • Anticipation Entry: Enter inside the pattern with early confirmation

Breakout = aggressive Retest = conservative Anticipation = advanced
