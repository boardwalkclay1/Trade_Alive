Stage 26 – Candle Traps

What Is a Candle Trap?

A candle trap is when a candle looks strong but is actually weak — or looks weak but is actually strong. This happens when traders react emotionally to a single bar instead of the full story.

A strong green candle at the top of a move can be a trap. A strong red candle into support can be a trap. A wick that looks scary can be a trap.

This is why discipline matters. You don’t react to one candle. You read the context.

Avoiding Traps with Structure

Moving averages, trend, and patterns protect you from candle traps. A strong green candle against the trend is not strength — it’s a trap. A strong red candle into rising support is not weakness — it’s a trap.

This is how you avoid emotional trades and stay aligned with your plan.
