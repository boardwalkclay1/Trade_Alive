Stage 36 – Candles at Key Levels

Candles React at Key Levels

Support and resistance levels are where the auction fights hardest. Candles at these levels reveal the truth: who is winning, who is losing, and whether the level will hold or break.

A rejection wick at resistance? Sellers defending. A strong green candle breaking resistance? Buyers attacking. A weak candle at support? Buyers hesitating.

Levels + Trend + MAs

Candles at key levels matter most when aligned with trend and moving averages. A strong candle breaking resistance in an uptrend is powerful. A strong candle breaking support in a downtrend is powerful.

This is how you confirm your ideas with discipline.
