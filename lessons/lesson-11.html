Stage 11 – Deeper Market Perspective

The Market as a Continuous Auction

The stock market is a live auction. Buyers place bids, sellers place asks, and price moves to wherever the most recent agreement happens. Every tick is someone saying: “Yes, I will pay that” or “Yes, I will sell for that.” 

LIVE AUCTION LADDER
──────────────────────────────────────────────

BUYERS (Bids)        CURRENT PRICE        SELLERS (Asks)
   98.50  ▲                                 ▲   98.55
   98.45  │                                 │   98.60
   98.40  │                                 │   98.65
          │                                 │
          └─────── Price moves up when buyers lift the ask
          └─────── Price moves down when sellers hit the bid

Thinking this way keeps you grounded. You are not trading magic lines — you are trading the constant negotiation between buyers and sellers.

What Ten Years Teaches You

Over ten years, I’ve watched this auction play out across bull markets, drops, sideways days, and chaotic news events. The $400 → $20,000 stretch didn’t happen because of luck — it happened because I stayed aligned with the auction.

The market doesn’t care how you feel. Your job is to read the auction, make a plan, and stick to it.

Connecting Auction View to Your Trades

Every time you consider a call or a put, ask: “Where is the auction leaning?” Are buyers slowly winning? Are sellers pressing? Or is the market balanced and undecided?
