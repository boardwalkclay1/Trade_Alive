Stage 55 – Double Tops

What a Double Top Is

A double top is a bearish reversal pattern. Price pushes into a resistance level, rejects, pulls back, then tries again — and fails at the same level.

This shows buyer exhaustion and seller strength.

Double Top Requirements

  • Strong uptrend into resistance
  • First rejection
  • Pullback
  • Second rejection at the same level
  • Break of neckline (confirmation)

The neckline break is the real signal — not the two tops.
