Stage 19 – Candle Foundations

What Green & White Bars Really Mean

In this chapter, we break down the most basic but most powerful visual in trading: the green and white bars — your candles. These bars tell the story of buyers and sellers in real time. They show aggression, hesitation, momentum, and exhaustion.

A green bar means buyers stepped up. A white (or hollow) bar means buyers pushed price up from the open. A red bar means sellers took control. These bars are not random — they are the heartbeat of the auction you learned about in earlier chapters.

When I turned $400 into $40,000 in five days, candles were one of the main things I watched. Not because they predict the future, but because they show the truth of the moment.

Candles Tell a Story

Every candle has a body and wicks. The body shows where price opened and closed. The wicks show where price tried to go but couldn’t stay. This is the story of pressure — who is winning, who is losing, and where the fight is happening.

Candles connect directly to your moving averages, your patterns, and your trend structure. They confirm or deny your trade ideas. They help you avoid emotional entries and guide your discipline.
