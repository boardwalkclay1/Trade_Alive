Stage 32 – Candle Location

Location Changes Everything

A candle means nothing without location. A strong green candle at the top of a move is dangerous. A strong green candle at a moving average in trend is healthy.

Location is the difference between a disciplined entry and an emotional mistake.

Location + Trend + MAs

Candles near moving averages matter more. Candles at support or resistance matter more. Candles inside patterns matter more.

This is how you avoid traps and stay aligned with your plan.
