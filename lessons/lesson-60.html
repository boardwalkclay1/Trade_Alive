Stage 60 – Breakout Traps

What a Trap Is

A breakout or breakdown trap happens when price breaks a pattern, triggers emotional traders, then snaps back in the opposite direction.

Traps expose emotional entries and reward disciplined patience.

Trap Behavior

  • Breakout wick
  • Instant reversal
  • Return inside pattern
  • Breakout in opposite direction

Candles tell the truth faster than the pattern itself.
