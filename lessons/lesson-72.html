Stage 72 – Pattern-Based Trade Building

Patterns Become Trades Through Process

A pattern is not a trade. A pattern becomes a trade when it aligns with your full system:

  • Trend
  • Location
  • Structure
  • Moving averages
  • Volume
  • Candles
  • Risk management

This is how disciplined traders build consistent setups.

The Pattern Trade Formula

Every pattern‑based trade follows this formula:

  • Identify the trend
  • Locate the pattern
  • Validate with MAs + volume
  • Confirm with candles
  • Enter only on confirmation
  • Manage risk with structure

This is the disciplined way to trade patterns.
