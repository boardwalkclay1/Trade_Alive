Stage 71 – Pattern Psychology

Patterns Are Human Behavior

Patterns form because humans behave the same way under pressure. Buyers get excited, sellers get scared, traders chase, traders hesitate — and the chart records all of it.

Patterns are the visual result of:

  • Fear
  • Greed
  • Hesitation
  • Exhaustion
  • Accumulation
  • Distribution

Understanding psychology makes patterns predictable — not the shape, but the behavior.

Psychology Behind Each Pattern Type

  • Flags: Trend resting
  • Pennants: Pressure tightening
  • Wedges: One side weakening
  • Triangles: Energy building
  • Double tops/bottoms: Failure at key levels
  • H&S: Exhaustion + takeover
  • Traps: Emotional traders getting baited

Patterns are psychology made visible.
