Stage 51 – Pennants

What a Pennant Is

A pennant is a continuation pattern that forms after a strong move, just like a flag — but instead of a channel, price compresses into a small triangle.

This shows buyers and sellers tightening up before the next expansion.

Pennant Requirements

  • Strong trend move (the pole)
  • Compression into a triangle
  • Lower highs + higher lows
  • Small candles
  • Breakout in trend direction

Pennants are powerful when combined with candle confirmation.
