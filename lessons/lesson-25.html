Stage 25 – Multi‑Candle Storytelling

Candles Are Chapters, Not Words

A single candle is a word. A sequence of candles is a sentence. A full trend is a story. Traders who only look at one candle miss the bigger message. You’ve already learned how moving averages show trend and structure — now you learn how candles reveal the emotional rhythm inside that trend.

Three strong green candles in a row? Buyers pressing. A strong green followed by hesitation? Buyers slowing. A rejection wick after momentum? Pressure shifting.

This is how you read the auction with clarity instead of guessing.

Sequence > Single Candle

A bullish engulfing candle means more when it appears after compression. A rejection wick means more when it appears at a moving average. A strong candle means more when it aligns with trend.

This is why your discipline matters. You don’t react to one bar. You read the sequence.
