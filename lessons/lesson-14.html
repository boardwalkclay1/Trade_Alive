Stage 14 – Chapter 2 Mock Trade

Bringing Chapter 2 Together

In Chapter 2, you studied the market as an auction, charts and candles, timeframes, patterns, moving averages, and how these elements combine into one setup.

Now you will walk through a mock trade using this bigger picture. This is not about guessing perfectly—it is about showing that you can think clearly and build a structured idea from the tools you’ve learned.

Just like the final stage of Chapter 1, this is a mental rehearsal. You are training your process and your discipline before any real money is at risk.
