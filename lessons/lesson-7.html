Stage 7 – What the Market Really Is

Seeing the Market Through Candles

Every chart you will ever read is built from one simple building block: the candlestick. Each candle shows a battle between buyers and sellers during a specific timeframe. 

CANDLESTICK ANATOMY
──────────────────────────────────────────────

   GREEN CANDLE (Buyers winning)

           High
            │
            │      ┌───────┐
            │      │       │  ← Body (Open → Close)
            │      │       │
            │      └───────┘
            │         ▲
            │         │ Close
            │         │
            │         │ Open
            ▼
           Low

   RED CANDLE (Sellers winning)

           High
            │
            │      ┌───────┐
            │      │       │
            │      │       │  ← Body (Close → Open)
            │      └───────┘
            │         ▲
            │         │ Open
            │         │
            │         │ Close
            ▼
           Low

Green means buyers pushed price upward. Red means sellers pushed price downward. These candles stack into trends, reversals, and structures you’ll learn next.

The Market Behind the Contracts

Every options contract sits on top of a deeper system: the live auction of the stock market. Every tick is a negotiation between buyers and sellers. 

LIVE AUCTION FLOW
──────────────────────────────────────────────

   BUYERS (Bids)  ← competing to lift price
        ▲
        │
        │     Price moves up when buyers outbid sellers
        │
        ▼
   CURRENT PRICE
        ▲
        │
        │     Price moves down when sellers undercut buyers
        │
        ▼
   SELLERS (Asks)  ← competing to push price lower

When you choose a direction, a call, or a put, you are stepping into this auction.

A Decade of Experience, Condensed for You

I have been in this environment for over ten years. I have seen accounts grow, accounts shrink, strategies work and then stop working. I have experienced long stretches of discipline and painful periods of emotional trading.

One of the most intense stretches of my journey was turning $400 into $20,000 in five days. On the surface, that sounds like a pure “knowledge” story. In reality, it was a discipline story: following my rules, respecting my risk, and not letting greed pull me out of my plan.

The most important part of trading is not the knowledge, the patterns, or the indicators. It is your discipline and your control over yourself while you use them.

How This Connects Back to Chapter 1

In Chapter 1, you learned to think in simple terms: money in → direction → money out. Now you’ll see how charts reveal that direction through structure. 

TREND STRUCTURE (Higher Highs / Higher Lows)
──────────────────────────────────────────────

      ↑        ↑        ↑
      HH       HH       HH
       \        \        \
        \        \        \
         \        \        \
          HL       HL       HL
           ↑        ↑        ↑

      Buyers are in control → Uptrend

REVERSAL (Lower Highs / Lower Lows)
──────────────────────────────────────────────

      ↓        ↓        ↓
      LH       LH       LH
       /        /        /
      /        /        /
     /        /        /
    LL       LL       LL
     ↓        ↓        ↓

      Sellers are in control → Downtrend

These structures are the foundation of reading charts, and they directly support your directional decisions.
