Stage 70 – Pattern Trading Mistakes

Most Traders Lose Because of This

Patterns don’t fail — traders fail patterns. Most traders lose because they treat patterns like predictions instead of context.

These are the biggest mistakes emotional traders make:

  • Trading patterns without trend
  • Trading patterns without confirmation
  • Trading patterns at the wrong location
  • Chasing breakouts
  • Ignoring volume
  • Ignoring moving averages
  • Forcing patterns that aren’t there

Discipline eliminates these mistakes.

The Discipline Checklist

  • Trend first
  • Location second
  • Structure third
  • Volume fourth
  • Candles confirm

This is how you avoid emotional pattern trading.
