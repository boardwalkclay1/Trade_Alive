Stage 61 – Continuation Logic

What Continuation Logic Really Is

Continuation logic explains why trends pause, compress, then continue. Patterns like flags, pennants, channels, and triangles are not random — they are the market catching its breath.

Continuation patterns show healthy trend structure and controlled pullbacks.

Continuation Requirements

  • Strong trend direction
  • Controlled pullback or compression
  • Small candles during the pause
  • Breakout in trend direction
  • Candle confirmation

Continuation is a system — not a guess.
