Stage 42 – Liquidity Grabs

What Is a Liquidity Grab?

A liquidity grab happens when price spikes above or below a key level to trigger stops, then snaps back instantly. These candles have long wicks and fast reversals.

Emotional traders get trapped. Disciplined traders wait for the snap‑back.

Candle Behavior During Liquidity Grabs

  • Long wick through a key level
  • Fast reversal
  • Small body
  • Often forms at support/resistance

This is one of the clearest signs of manipulation — and opportunity.
