Stage 10 – Moving Averages & Trade Framework

What Moving Averages Show You

A moving average is a line on your chart that smooths out price over a chosen period (like 9, 20, or 50 candles). It helps you see the general direction and strength of a trend without getting lost in every tiny move.

When price is generally above a rising moving average, buyers have often been in control recently. When price is generally below a falling moving average, sellers have often been in control.

Moving averages are not magic buy and sell buttons. They are context tools that help you answer: “Is this trend mostly up, mostly down, or undecided right now?”

Tying It All Together

By this point you have seen: the market as an auction, charts and candles, basic patterns, and now moving averages. Alone, each piece is incomplete. Together, they can support one solid trade idea.

A strong trade might include: a clear direction, supportive candles, a helpful pattern, alignment with a moving average trend, and—most important—your discipline and risk plan.

Your goal is not to be perfect. Your goal is to build a repeatable process where all these pieces work together, and where you control yourself more than you try to control the market.
