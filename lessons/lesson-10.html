Stage 10 – Moving Averages & Trade Framework

What Moving Averages Show You

A moving average is a line on your chart that smooths out price over a chosen period (like 9, 20, or 50 candles). It helps you see the general direction and strength of a trend without getting lost in every tiny move. 

MOVING AVERAGE VISUAL
──────────────────────────────────────────────

Price ▲
      │        ││   ││     ││
      │        ││   ││     ││
      │   ~~~~~~ 9 MA (fast)
      │      ──────── 20 MA (medium)
      │         ──────────── 50 MA (slow)
      │
      ▼

Interpretation:
9 MA → reacts quickly to price  
20 MA → shows short‑term trend  
50 MA → shows bigger picture trend

When price is above a rising moving average, buyers have often been in control. When price is below a falling moving average, sellers have often been in control.

Moving averages are not magic buy/sell buttons — they are context tools that help you answer: “Is this trend mostly up, mostly down, or undecided right now?”

Tying It All Together

By this point you have seen: the market as an auction, charts and candles, basic patterns, and now moving averages. Alone, each piece is incomplete. Together, they can support one solid trade idea.

A strong trade might include: • clear direction • supportive candles • a helpful pattern • alignment with a moving average trend • and most important — your discipline and risk plan

Your goal is not perfection. Your goal is a repeatable process where all these pieces work together.

The Full Trade Framework

FULL TRADE FRAMEWORK
──────────────────────────────────────────────

1. Direction
      ↑ Up or ↓ Down from your entry

2. Candles
      Strong green? Strong red? Higher lows? Lower highs?

3. Pattern
      Flag? Wedge? Double bottom? Double top?

4. Moving Average Trend
      Price above rising MA? Below falling MA?

5. Risk Plan
      Where do you exit if wrong?

6. Execution
      Market or limit? Quantity? Discipline?

When all pieces align → you have a structured trade idea.
