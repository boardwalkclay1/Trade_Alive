Stage 29 – Continuation Signals

Continuation Candles

Continuation candles show that the trend is still healthy. These include:

  • Small pullback candles with small wicks
  • Inside bars near moving averages
  • Strong candles after compression

These candles don’t predict — they confirm that the trend is still intact.

Continuation + Moving Averages

Continuation signals matter most when they appear near rising or falling moving averages. A small pullback candle into a rising MA? Healthy. A small pullback candle into a falling MA? Healthy for sellers.

This is how you stay aligned with your plan instead of guessing.
