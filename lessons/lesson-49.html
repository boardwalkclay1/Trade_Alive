Stage 49 – What Chart Patterns Are

Chart Patterns = Market Psychology

Chart patterns are not magic shapes. They are the visual result of buyers and sellers fighting over time. Patterns show pressure building, pressure releasing, or pressure reversing.

Every pattern is built from:

  • Trend direction
  • Structure (higher highs, lower lows)
  • Moving averages
  • Candlestick behavior
  • Key levels

Patterns don’t predict — they organize the auction so you can read it with discipline.

Continuation vs Reversal Patterns

There are only two types of patterns:

  • Continuation – trend pauses, then continues
  • Reversal – trend weakens, then flips

Everything else is just a variation of these two.
