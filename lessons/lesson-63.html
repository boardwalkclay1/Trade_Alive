Stage 63 – Pattern + Candle Integration

Patterns Don’t Work Alone

A pattern is just structure. A candle is just a bar. But when you combine them with trend, moving averages, and levels — you get a complete system.

Patterns show pressure. Candles show execution. Together they show intent.

Integration Checklist

  • Trend direction
  • Pattern structure
  • Moving average alignment
  • Candle confirmation
  • Breakout or failure logic

This is how disciplined traders build consistent setups.
