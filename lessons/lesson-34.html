Stage 34 – Candle Volume Interaction

Why Volume Matters with Candles

A candle shows direction and pressure. Volume shows participation. When a strong candle forms with strong volume, it means real buyers or sellers stepped in. When a strong candle forms with weak volume, it may be a trap.

This is how you separate real strength from fake strength — and avoid emotional trades.

Volume + Trend + MAs

A strong green candle at a moving average with rising volume? Healthy continuation. A strong red candle breaking below a moving average with rising volume? Real weakness. A candle with no volume? Noise.

This is how you confirm your ideas with discipline.
