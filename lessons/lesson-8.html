Stage 8 – Charts & Candles

What a Chart Really Is

A chart is a visual record of how price has moved over time. It is not a prediction device; it is a history book written in real time. Every bar or candle is the result of buyers and sellers interacting in the market.

When you look at a chart, you are seeing where buyers stepped in, where sellers pushed back, and where the balance of power shifted. It is a map of the auction you learned in Stage 7.

Red and Green Candles

Most trading platforms show price using candlesticks. A green candle means price closed higher than it opened. A red candle means price closed lower than it opened. 

CANDLE ANATOMY
──────────────────────────────────────────────

GREEN CANDLE (Buyers in control)

           High
            │
            │      │
            │      │   ← Wick
            │   ┌───────┐
            │   │       │  ← Body (Open → Close)
            │   └───────┘
            │      │
            │      │
           Low

RED CANDLE (Sellers in control)

           High
            │
            │      │
            │      │
            │   ┌───────┐
            │   │       │  ← Body (Close → Open)
            │   └───────┘
            │      │
            │      │
           Low

The body shows the open and close. The wicks show the highest and lowest prices reached. Together, they tell a story about pressure.

  • Strong green candle: buyers were in control.
  • Strong red candle: sellers were in control.
  • Long wicks: rejection or hesitation.

Candles Tell a Story

A single candle is one moment in the auction. A series of candles becomes a story: higher lows, lower highs, momentum, hesitation, pressure building, pressure releasing. 

CANDLE STORY SEQUENCE
──────────────────────────────────────────────

        │
     ┌───────┐
     │       │
     └───────┘

Candle 2: Higher low, buyers stepping in earlier

       ┌───────┐
       │       │
       └───────┘

Interpretation:
→ Buyers showing strength
→ Momentum building
→ Pressure lifting price

When you combine candles with the auction mindset, you begin to see the market as a living negotiation instead of random noise.
