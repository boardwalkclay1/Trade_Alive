Stage 67 – Pattern + Volume Integration

Volume Reveals Commitment

Patterns show structure. Candles show execution. But volume shows commitment. When a pattern breaks with strong volume, it means real money is behind the move.

Low‑volume breakouts are weak. High‑volume breakouts are strong.

Volume Rules

  • Continuation patterns break with rising volume
  • Reversal patterns break with volume spikes
  • Traps break with low volume then reverse with high volume
  • Fakeouts often have weak volume

Volume is the lie detector of chart patterns.
