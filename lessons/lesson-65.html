Stage 65 – Pattern Location

Location Determines Meaning

A pattern at the wrong location is meaningless. A bull flag at resistance is not the same as a bull flag at support. A head & shoulders in the middle of a trend is not the same as one at major resistance.

Location gives the pattern its purpose.

Key Locations

  • Support – bullish reversals
  • Resistance – bearish reversals
  • Moving averages – continuation zones
  • Trendlines – structure confirmation
  • Breakout levels – expansion zones

Patterns without location are just drawings.
