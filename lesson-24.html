Stage 24 – Candle Confirmation

Candles + Trend + MAs = Clarity

This stage brings everything together: candles, moving averages, trend structure, and your discipline. A candle pattern inside a trend, supported by moving averages, is far more powerful than a candle pattern alone.

This is how you avoid emotional trades. This is how you stay consistent. This is how you build the discipline that separates gamblers from traders.

Confirmation vs. Guessing

A candle pattern confirms your idea — it does not create it. You start with trend. You start with structure. You start with moving averages. Then you use candles to confirm or deny your plan.

This is the same process you used in the mock enter/exit simulator. You build the idea first, then you confirm it with real evidence.
