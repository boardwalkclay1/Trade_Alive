Stage 76 – Trade Management

Trade Management Is Discipline

Getting into a trade is easy. Managing it is where discipline shows. Trade management must follow structure — not emotion.

Your job after entering a pattern‑based trade:

  • Protect the stop
  • Monitor structure
  • Watch volume behavior
  • Track candle strength
  • Follow your target plan

Emotional management destroys trades. Structured management protects them.

Management Rules

  • Continuation trades: Hold as long as structure stays intact
  • Reversal trades: Reduce risk quickly after confirmation
  • Breakout trades: Watch for trap signals
  • Retest trades: Tighten stops after retest holds

Management is dynamic — but never emotional.
