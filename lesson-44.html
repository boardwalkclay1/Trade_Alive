Stage 44 – Trend Exhaustion Candles

What Trend Exhaustion Looks Like

Trend exhaustion is when a trend runs out of energy. Candles shrink, wicks grow, and momentum fades. This is the market telling you the move is tired.

Exhaustion is not reversal — it’s a warning.

Exhaustion + Moving Averages

Exhaustion far from moving averages is dangerous — overextension. Exhaustion near moving averages can be healthy — a pause before continuation.

This is how you avoid emotional entries and protect profits.
