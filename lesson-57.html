Stage 57 – Head & Shoulders

What Head & Shoulders Is

Head & Shoulders is the most powerful bearish reversal pattern. It forms when buyers push up, fail, push higher, fail again, then push lower than before.

It shows buyer exhaustion and seller takeover.

Pattern Structure

  • Left shoulder
  • Head (higher high)
  • Right shoulder (lower high)
  • Neckline
  • Breakdown (confirmation)

The neckline break is the real signal — not the shoulders.
