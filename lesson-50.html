Stage 50 – Flag Patterns

What a Flag Pattern Is

A flag is a continuation pattern that forms after a strong move. Price pulls back in a tight, controlled channel — like a flag on a pole.

The “pole” is the strong move. The “flag” is the controlled pullback.

Flag Requirements

  • Strong trend move (the pole)
  • Tight pullback channel
  • Small candles
  • Low volatility
  • Breakout in trend direction

Flags are one of the highest‑quality continuation patterns when combined with moving averages.
