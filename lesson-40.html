Stage 40 – Consolidation Candles

What Consolidation Really Is

Consolidation is when the market moves sideways — neither buyers nor sellers have control. Candles become small, wicks appear on both sides, and price stays inside a tight range.

This is where emotional traders get chopped up. But disciplined traders wait.

Candle Behavior in Consolidation

  • Small bodies
  • Wicks on both ends
  • Inside bars
  • Repeated rejection at the same levels

These candles show balance — not opportunity.
