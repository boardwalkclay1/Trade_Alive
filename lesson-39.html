Stage 39 – Pullback Candles

Healthy Pullbacks

Healthy pullbacks have small candles, small wicks, and controlled movement. They often form near rising moving averages in an uptrend or falling moving averages in a downtrend.

These pullbacks show that the trend is intact and buyers or sellers are simply taking a breath.

Dangerous Pullbacks

Dangerous pullbacks have large candles, long wicks, and aggressive movement. They often break moving averages or key structure levels.

These pullbacks show that the trend may be weakening — or reversing.
