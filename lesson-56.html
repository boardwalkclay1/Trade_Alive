Stage 56 – Double Bottoms

What a Double Bottom Is

A double bottom is a bullish reversal pattern. Price sells into support, rejects, pulls back, then tests the level again — and holds.

This shows seller exhaustion and buyer strength.

Double Bottom Requirements

  • Strong downtrend into support
  • First rejection
  • Pullback
  • Second rejection at the same level
  • Break of neckline (confirmation)

The neckline break is the real signal — not the two bottoms.
