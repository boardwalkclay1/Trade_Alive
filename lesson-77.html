Stage 77 – Scaling Into Trades

Scaling Must Be Earned

Scaling is not adding size randomly — it is adding size only when the trade proves itself.

Emotional traders scale because they “feel confident.” Disciplined traders scale because the chart earns it.

Scaling Rules

  • Scale only after confirmation
  • Scale only into strength
  • Never scale into weakness
  • Scale at structure breaks
  • Scale at retests that hold

Scaling is a reward — not a gamble.
