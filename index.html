How This Academy Works

This platform is a discipline-first training environment for aspiring options traders. We do not tell you what to trade. We train how you show up, how you prepare, and how you execute. Every part of this experience is designed to build timing, consistency, and emotional control.

  1. Daily 9:00 AM Check-In: Monday through Friday, you must check in before the market opens. This trains you to be prepared on time. Missed check-ins break your streak and lower your discipline score.
  2. Mock Options Registration: Before you can access any lessons, you must complete a simulated options trading application. This teaches you what real brokers look for—without risking anything.
  3. Locked Lesson Stages: Lessons are broken into stages. Each stage unlocks only when you complete the one before it. You cannot skip ahead. Discipline and completion are required.
  4. Simulated Execution: You will practice entering and exiting trades in a fully simulated environment. You will see how quantity, bid price, and timing affect your outcomes.
  5. Emotional Control Training: As you progress, you will work on managing fear, greed, and impatience—because undisciplined emotions are the fastest way to lose in the market.

This is not a get-rich-quick platform. This is an academy for people who are willing to show up, follow instructions, and build real habits.
