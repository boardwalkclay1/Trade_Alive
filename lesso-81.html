Stage 81 – Pattern Drills

Drills Build Automatic Skill

Drills turn knowledge into instinct. These exercises train your eyes, your discipline, and your execution.

Drill Types

  • Pattern Identification Drill: Identify 20 patterns from screenshots
  • Trend Alignment Drill: Label trend before labeling pattern
  • Location Drill: Mark support/resistance before analyzing pattern
  • Confirmation Drill: Identify confirmation candles
  • Failure Drill: Identify traps and failed breakouts

Repetition builds mastery.
